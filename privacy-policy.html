Privacy Policy — Bang Bang BGD

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Bang Bang BGD does not sell or share personal data and does not use it for advertising or any purpose other than the features described below.

Data stored on your device

Applicant profiles, passport photos, uploaded documents and settings are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage / IndexedDB). They are not uploaded to us unless you use a feature below.

Optional features that send data

  • Google Gemini AI assist: if you enter your own API key, the document image you choose to process is sent to Google's Generative Language API. Google's terms apply.
  • OCR.space: if you enter your own key, the document image you choose to process is sent to OCR.space.
  • Access key / cloud features: your access key and the minimum data needed to validate it, track credits and deliver OTP codes are exchanged with our Firebase backend.

We do not collect browsing history. The extension only runs on indianvisa-bangladesh.nic.in and indianvisaonline.gov.in.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes all locally stored data. To delete data held on our server, contact us on WhatsApp.
