Privacy Policy — AB Indian Visa Auto Fill

Last updated: 20 November 2026

AB Indian Visa Auto Fill (also shown inside the extension as “Bang Bang BGD”) does not sell personal data and does not use it for advertising, creditworthiness or any purpose other than the features described below.

Data we handle

Applicant details you enter or extract (name, date of birth, passport number, address, phone, email, photo, uploaded passport / visa documents) and your access key.

Data stored on your device

Applicant profiles, passport photos, uploaded documents and settings are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage / IndexedDB). Built-in OCR runs on your device. This data is not uploaded to us unless you use a feature below.

Optional features that send data

  • Google Gemini AI assist: if you enter your own API key, the document image you choose to process is sent to Google's Generative Language API. Google's terms apply.
  • OCR.space: if you enable it, the document image you choose to process is sent, through our processing service, to OCR.space. Google/OCR.space terms apply.
  • Access key / cloud features: your access key and the minimum data needed to validate it, track credits and deliver one-time codes (OTP) are exchanged with our Firebase backend.
  • Autofill: the profile data you choose is entered only into the Indian visa application website you are using.

We do not collect browsing history. The extension only runs on indianvisa-bangladesh.nic.in and indianvisaonline.gov.in. We do not transfer user data to third parties except as described above to provide the feature you chose, and we do not use it for any unrelated purpose.

Deleting your data

Removing the extension deletes all locally stored data. To delete data held on our server, contact us.
